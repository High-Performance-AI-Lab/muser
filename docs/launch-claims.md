# Launch claims register

its current evidence and the wording it may carry only after the same result
is reproduced by the exact-identity atomic final bundle. If a proposed line
of launch copy is not a plain restatement of an approved-wording row, it does
not ship — take it back to `docs/release-provenance.md` for a new evidence
citation first. `[measured]` / `[precedent-7B-ferrite]` / `[target]` /
`[roadmap]` / `[mock]` follow the legend in `docs/metrics-schema.md`.
Rows marked **OPERATOR REVIEW REQUIRED** contain evidence-backed proposed
wording that is not owner-approved. It remains unavailable to launch copy even
if its reproduction gate later passes, until the operator approves it.

## Explicitly post-launch (not in v1)

These are known, intentional gaps in the one-button onboarding flow (claim
#9) and the underlying disaggregated topology (claim #8). They are not
"coming soon" launch copy — they simply do not exist yet and must not be
implied:

- **Node discovery.** `Add node` takes a manually entered `user@host`; there
  is no LAN scan, mDNS, or inventory import.
- **Multi-node scheduling.** Onboarding more than one node registers each
  one, but the serving topology stays single-producer (1x Mac + 1x GX10,
  claim #8) — there is no scheduler picking among multiple healthy prefill
  nodes.
- **Revocation.** There is no supported `muser node remove` / de-enroll
  flow that walks back a node's certificates, HMAC key, or registry entry.
- **Deep reuse coverage remains partial.** The cold text TTFT matrix covers
  8,192, 16,384, 32,768, 65,536, and the 130,815-token class. Warm reuse is
  measured at 65,536 and 130,815 tokens, delta handoff at 65,536, and the
  controlled EEE-off cold cell at 130,815 (claims #6, #11, #12). Reuse and
  delta are not measured at every depth, and every deep multimodal cell
  remains unrun. No broad long-context wording may exceed those claim rows.
- **Multimodal remote cells and send-during-prefill.** Remote multimodal
  handoffs are unqualified, and connector streaming during prefill was
  analyzed and deferred (the wire schedule's strict tile order means no
  segment can leave before the last NoPE layer computes). Neither may be
  implied.

See `docs/one-button-onboarding.md` "v1 limits" for the full, current list.

## Ground rules for anyone drafting launch copy

- The release lock (`release/release-lock.json`) is authoritative: while the
  feature contract is in containment, no row above goes live regardless of
  how strong its evidence is. Conditional wordings activate only when the
  contract leaves containment and the row's stated reproduction gate passes.
- A number with no row above does not ship. Add a row (with its evidence
  citation) before using it.
- `[precedent-7B-ferrite]` numbers (e.g. 34.9/308 t/s, 21.9–30.1x restore,
  24.6 GB/s fabric, 1.42x ANE+GPU concurrency) describe the historical Ferrite
  research lineage on a 7B model, never this Muser program. They may appear in
  engineering docs as context but never as a Muser product claim.
- "Measured" in this document means measured on Muser, this program, under a
  retained receipt or seal — not "measured once, on any hardware, ever."
- When evidence and wording conflict, evidence wins and the wording row gets
  corrected — copy is never allowed to outrun the receipt.
